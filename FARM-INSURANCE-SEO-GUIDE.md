## Step 2: Configure Rank Math SEO

### A. Update the SEO Title

In the **Rank Math SEO** box:

1. **Click "Edit Snippet"** in the preview section
2. **SEO Title:** 
   ```
   Farm Insurance Sioux Falls SD | Farm & Ranch Insurance South Dakota
   ```
   (Make sure it includes your focus keywords!)

### B. Add Meta Description

1. In the same **Edit Snippet** area:
2. **Meta Description:**
   ```
   Comprehensive farm & ranch insurance in Sioux Falls, SD. Protect your farmhouse, barns, equipment, livestock, and liability. Get free quotes from Sperling Insurance, serving South Dakota farms since 20+ years.
   ```
   (Include focus keywords naturally)

### C. Update the URL Slug

1. Click **"Edit"** next to the permalink
2. Change from `/farm-insurance/` to:
   ```
   /farm-insurance-sioux-falls-south-dakota/
   ```
   OR
   ```
   /farm-ranch-insurance-sioux-falls/
   ```
   (Include focus keywords in the URL)

### D. Add Focus Keywords (Already Done!)

You already have:

**Keep these!** Make sure they appear:
- In the SEO title (✅ done with the title above)

### E. Verify Content Structure

## Step 3: Additional Rank Math Settings

### Schema Tab
1. Go to **Schema** tab in Rank Math
2. **Schema Type:** Select "WebPage" or "FAQPage" (FAQPage is already added via template)
3. The template already includes FAQ schema - you're good here!

### Advanced Tab
1. **Canonical URL:** Leave blank (will use page URL)
2. **Robots Meta:**
   - ✅ Index this page (checked)
   - ❌ Noindex (unchecked)
   - ❌ Nofollow (unchecked)
